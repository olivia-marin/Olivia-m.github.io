What it is

A pressed powder blush with a true-matte velvet finish. The square compact mirrors the architectural lines of the collection, with a frosted glass base, weighted black lid, and a pan of colour you can see through the packaging.

How to use

Swirl a fluffy brush in the pan, tap off excess, and sweep onto the apples of your cheeks toward the temple. For a sun-kissed look, dust lightly across the bridge of the nose. Pair with Dewy Lip & Cheek Tint for a monochromatic flush.

Shade guide

Dusty Rose (07) is our bestseller, a cool mauve that suits most undertones. Warm skin? Try Terracotta. Deep skin? Berry and Plum were developed with richer pigment load so colour stays true, not ashy.
