What it is

A lightweight liquid foundation with medium, buildable coverage and a natural satin finish. Formulated with hyaluronic acid and squalane so skin looks hydrated, not flat, for up to 12 hours.

How to use

Shake well. Apply one pump to the back of your hand, then blend outward from the center of your face using fingers, a damp sponge, or our Pro Fusion Brush. Add a second layer only where you want more coverage.

Key ingredients

  • Hyaluronic acid: draws moisture into skin for a plump, smooth look
  • Squalane: helps prevent dryness without a greasy feel
  • Vitamin E: antioxidant support for comfortable wear
