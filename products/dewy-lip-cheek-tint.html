What it is

A multi-use liquid tint with a glossy-dewy finish. The slim bottle fits in any bag; the doe-foot applicator makes it easy to dot on cheeks and lips without a brush.

How to use

Dab one dot on each cheek and blend with fingertips while the product is still wet. Press a thin layer onto lips for a stained effect, or layer for more intensity. Set cheeks with Velvet Matte Blush on top for longer wear.
