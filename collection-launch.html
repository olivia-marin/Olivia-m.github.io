Beauty Pass × TerraCycle

Made from empties you returned

This collection was born from the Sephora × TerraCycle programme. The empty products customers brought back in store were recycled and remade into the formulas and packaging you see in this launch.

Beauty recycled

Through TerraCycle bins in our stores, Beauty Pass members returned clean, empty packaging from across the beauty aisle, including bottles, compacts, tubes, and caps that are usually hard to recycle at home.

Beauty redeemed

Those returns were sorted, processed, and recovered. Plastic, glass, and metal were reclaimed from the empties you dropped off, and every eligible return earned Beauty Pass points along the way.

Beauty made new

That recovered material helped shape this five product line: new Sephora Collection formulas and frosted glass packaging, designed as a complete complexion and lip routine built from what came back.
