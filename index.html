Beauty Pass × TerraCycle

Recycle empties. Earn points.

Sephora partners with TerraCycle to recycle hard-to-recycle beauty packaging. Join the programme, drop off your empties in store, and earn Beauty Pass points while keeping waste out of landfill.

  1. 1

    Join Beauty Pass

    Sign up free online or in store. You need to be a member to earn points on recycling.

  2. 2

    Empty your packaging

    Bring clean, empty cosmetic, skincare, or haircare units from any brand. Items must be fully empty and meet Sephora and TerraCycle safety standards.

  3. 3

    Drop off in store

    Take your empties to a participating Sephora store and place them in the Sephora × TerraCycle Beauty Waste Bin. Point-eligible drop-offs are limited to once per day.

  4. 4

    Earn Beauty Pass points

    Points are added to your account within up to 14 business days after your in-store return.
